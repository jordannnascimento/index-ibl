Services

What clients can engage Index-IBL to do.

Index-IBL offers a focused set of services designed to help buyers move from sourcing need to sourcing execution with more control, better visibility, and stronger manufacturer fit.

Each service can stand on its own. Together, they create a more dependable sourcing program.

01

Supplier Identification & Development

When buyers need more than a broad supplier search, this is where the work starts.

Index-IBL helps identify manufacturers that are relevant to the product, the category, and the requirements of the sourcing program — then helps move the process toward a more usable supplier base.

What the client gains
  • better-fit manufacturers
  • a more disciplined starting point
  • less wasted time in supplier search
  • stronger foundations for decision-making
What this service supports
  • supplier search across selected Latin American markets
  • initial screening and fit assessment
  • supplier development support
  • stronger sourcing foundations
02

Sourcing Project Support

A sourcing opportunity becomes more useful when it is organized, followed through, and kept moving.

Index-IBL supports buyers that need help turning an idea, requirement, or category initiative into a sourcing program with more structure and continuity.

What the client gains
  • clearer project momentum
  • better communication flow
  • less fragmentation
  • stronger continuity across the sourcing process
What this service supports
  • sourcing project structuring
  • supplier engagement and follow-up
  • coordination around requirements and timing
  • progress tracking and continuity support
03

Private Label & Product Development Support

Some sourcing programs require closer alignment between product goals and manufacturer reality.

Index-IBL supports product and private label initiatives that need sourcing feasibility, manufacturer coordination, and practical follow-up around specifications and execution.

What the client gains
  • a more structured path for differentiated product programs
  • better alignment between product intent and sourcing reality
  • stronger coordination with suitable manufacturers
What this service supports
  • product-development coordination
  • private label support
  • specification alignment
  • sourcing feasibility analysis
  • manufacturer coordination for differentiated programs
04

Quality & Supply-Chain Coordination

Supplier selection is only the beginning. Execution quality depends on what happens after the first decision.

Index-IBL supports buyers that need better follow-up, stronger visibility, and more reliable continuity once the sourcing program is active.

What the client gains
  • better execution visibility
  • stronger continuity
  • more dependable follow-up
  • improved supply rhythm across the process
What this service supports
  • supplier follow-up
  • quality coordination
  • production follow-up
  • supply-chain coordination
  • ongoing execution support

In selected situations, Index-IBL also supports broader international initiatives connected to sourcing, market development, and execution planning.

This remains a secondary capability and is activated where the context justifies it.

How the service model works as a whole

Some buyers come to Index-IBL for a specific need. Others build a broader sourcing relationship over time.

A program may begin with supplier identification, move into project support, require product-development coordination, and continue with quality and supply-chain follow-up.

That continuity is part of the value: support that can start where the need begins and remain useful as the program develops.

Looking for the right starting point?

If your company is assessing a sourcing need and wants to understand which kind of support is most relevant, Index-IBL is available to discuss it.
