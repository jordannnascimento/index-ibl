About

A sourcing office built for serious buying programs.

Index-IBL is an international sourcing and supply development company with more than 20 years of operating experience.

Its work has been shaped by demanding buying environments, long-term commercial relationships, and the day-to-day realities of running sourcing programs across multiple categories and markets.

The company did not grow out of theory. It grew out of sourcing work that required judgment, follow-through, and dependable execution.

What kind of company Index-IBL is

Index-IBL is built around a sourcing-office model.

That means its role goes beyond opening supplier conversations. The company is designed to help buyers move from market opportunity to manufacturer fit, and from manufacturer fit to a more coordinated sourcing program.

Its work typically sits where buyers need more structure than a simple introduction, and more commercial grounding than a generic intermediary can provide.

Experience that gives the model weight

Over the years, Index-IBL has worked with some of the largest companies in the world, across multiple categories and high-volume buying environments.

That experience includes sourcing and related support across the process, with operational activity involving thousands of containers every year.

This matters because it gives the model practical credibility. The company’s approach has been tested in environments where supplier fit, execution discipline, and continuity all matter.

Regional logic: broad enough to be useful, specific enough to be credible

Index-IBL’s public regional identity is Latin America.

Within that umbrella, its strongest execution depth is across South and Central America, including real sourcing experience in Mexico. This gives buyers a commercially useful regional frame while keeping the company grounded in operational reality.

The result is a sourcing model that combines regional reach, selective application, and real execution depth where it matters most.

Where the company is strongest

Index-IBL works across selected sourcing environments where its model can add value with credibility and discipline. Those grouped opportunity areas currently include:

  • Building, Improvement & Material Supply
  • Industrial & Manufacturing Supply
  • Food, Ingredients & Agro-Industrial Supply

These are sourcing environments where supplier qualification, requirement alignment, follow-up, and execution support can materially improve the result for the buyer.

Why buyers work with this kind of partner

Some sourcing programs fail because the opportunity was not there. Many fail because qualification was weak, alignment was poor, or follow-through was missing.

Index-IBL is built for buyers that want a more dependable sourcing relationship — one with stronger filtering, clearer coordination, and better continuity over time.

That is the role the company is designed to play.

In selected situations, Index-IBL also supports broader international initiatives related to sourcing, market development, and execution planning.

That work remains secondary. The company’s primary identity continues to be sourcing-office-centered and execution-led.
